In The News.

Since 6 May, Oxford Action for Palestine has transformed the conversation around Palestine on campus. We have forced public attention on Gaza through our encampment, rallies, direct action, and coalition building with students, faculty, staff, and city residents. We have worked to make sure that the University can no longer ignore its complicity in israeli genocide, occupation, and apartheid.
